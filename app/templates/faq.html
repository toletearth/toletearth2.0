Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about renting or listing on TOLET Earth.

Is TOLET Earth really free?

Yes. There's no fee to browse listings, no fee to post a property, and no brokerage charged on either side.

How are listings verified?

Each new listing is reviewed before it's published, to keep the details accurate and the platform trustworthy.

Which cities do you cover?

Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula, searchable right down to the sector.

How do I contact an owner?

Open any listing and use the "Call Owner" button — you're speaking to the owner directly, not an agent.

How do I list my property?

Go to "Post Property," fill in the details, and submit. It'll go live after a quick review.
