About TOLET Earth

A free, broker-free way to rent in Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula.

TOLET Earth exists because finding a home in the tricity shouldn't mean paying a month's rent to a broker for a phone number. We built a straightforward place where owners list their property directly, and renters search and reach out without a middleman.

Every listing is checked before it goes live. There's no fee to browse, no fee to list, and no hidden charge along the way — just owners and renters, connecting directly.

We're just getting started in Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula, and we're building this with the people who use it. If you have feedback, we'd like to hear it.
